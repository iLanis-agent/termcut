An extra $100 a month on your mortgage is not a tip. It's a term cut.

TermCut shows exactly what extra payments buy: how many months come off the loan, how much interest never gets charged, and whether a lump sum works harder as a recast or as a shorter term. Every claim comes with the amortization rows behind it.

The quiet math lenders don't headline

On a $400,000 loan at 6% for 30 years, adding $200/month erases about 65 months and keeps roughly $98,000 of interest in your pocket. TermCut computes your numbers, not the brochure's.

Recast or shorter term?

A lump sum can lower your monthly payment (recast) or keep the payment and end the loan early. Same money, very different outcomes. TermCut runs both side by side so the tradeoff is a number, not a vibe.

Proof, not vibes

Every figure is a full month-by-month amortization: balance curve, year-by-year table, and the exact schedule rows - first months, the lump-sum month, and the payoff month.
